BIOGRAPHY
A LIFE IN WORDS

The Mind Behindthe Stories

F

yodor Dostoevsky was born in Moscow in 1821 and became one of the most influential writers of the nineteenth century. His novels examined the complexities of human consciousness and the moral choices that shape a person's life.

His early life was marked by literature,political involvement and profound personal hardship. In 1849, he was arrested for participating in a literary discussion group and sentenced to death. The sentence was dramatically commuted at the last moment, followed by years of imprisonment and exile.

These experiences deeply influenced his later writing. His characters often struggle with guilt, freedom, faith, isolation and the possibility of redemption.

His novels remain among the most studied works of psychological and philosophical literature.
