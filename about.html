A STORY FROM OUR LAND

What remains
after the stone?

Places can disappear. Buildings can change. But stories have a way of remaining.

The Bamiyan Buddhas are part of a history that has been carried through generations. Their presence, absence and memory have become part of the cultural landscape of Afghanistan

Shamama takes inspiration from that idea: preserving what matters through stories, curiosity and the simple act of reading
